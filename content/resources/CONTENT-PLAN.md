# Resources content plan

## How this was researched, and its limits

- **What we looked at:**
  - Live search results and "people also ask" style questions on each topic.
  - The official help centres, which show the problems users actually hit.
  - Search-demand reporting that is publicly available.
- **What was not available:** this environment had no keyword tool, so there are no Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, Semrush or Search Console figures. Demand below is a relative judgement, **not measured volume**.
- **Next step:** validate the plan in Keyword Planner or Search Console once the site is verified, and re-prioritise using real impressions.

What the research showed:
- ChatGPT is by far the most searched AI tool, and interest in Claude is rising sharply.
- Searches are moving from "what is AI" to "how do I use it" and "why isn't it working".
- Troubleshooting pages that rank today are often thin, auto-generated lists. Accurate, well-sourced fixes have a realistic chance to outrank them.

How each topic was prioritised:
1. Relevance to Calon AI's services: automation, AI agents, integrations, data.
2. Clear search intent.
3. A realistic chance to rank against current results.
4. A natural route to a Calon AI service.

## Maintenance rules

- **Monthly:** review prices and plan details in the ChatGPT, Claude, Zapier, Make and n8n articles, and bump `updated:` after any material change.
- **News:** cite the primary announcement. Label vendor benchmark figures as the vendor's own claims. Never publish unverified rumours.
- **Once Search Console has data:** expand articles with impressions but a low click-through rate (improve titles and descriptions), and add sections for related queries they already rank for.
