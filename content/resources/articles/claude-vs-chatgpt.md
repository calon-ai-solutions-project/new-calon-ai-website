> **Quick answer:** For most small and medium businesses, either Claude or ChatGPT will handle everyday writing, summarising and analysis very well. Pick ChatGPT if you want the widest range of built-in features in one app. Pick Claude if your work involves long documents, careful instruction-following or software development with Claude Code. Whichever you choose, use a business plan for company data, and test both on your own tasks before deciding.

"Which AI should we use?" is one of the first questions business owners ask us. The honest answer is that the gap between the leading assistants is small for most everyday work, and it changes with each model release. What matters more is how well the tool fits your team and systems. Here is how to decide.

## At a glance

| | Claude (Anthropic) | ChatGPT (OpenAI) |
|---|---|---|
| Free plan | Yes, with usage limits | Yes, with usage limits |
| Team or business plan | Claude Team, from $20 per seat per month billed annually | ChatGPT Business, priced per user per month |
| Ongoing workspaces | Projects with knowledge and instructions | Projects with files and instructions |
| Reusable assistants | Projects and Skills | Custom GPTs |
| Documents and tools you can keep | Artifacts (docs, slides, dashboards, small apps) | Canvas and file outputs |
| Images | Diagrams and graphics through Artifacts | Built-in image generation and editing |

Prices and features as listed on each provider's official site in November 2026. Both change frequently: check [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing) and [chatgpt.com/pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) before buying.

## Where Claude tends to shine

- **Long documents.** Claude is widely used for reviewing contracts, policies, tenders and reports, and its Projects can hold large reference libraries on paid plans.
- **Following detailed instructions.** If you have a house style or a strict format, Claude is good at sticking to it.
- **Artifacts.** Claude can build documents, slide decks, dashboards and small interactive tools beside the chat, which you can edit and share.
- **Software development.** Claude Code works across the terminal, VS Code, JetBrains, a desktop app and the web, and is a common choice for teams building with AI.

## Where ChatGPT tends to shine

- **Breadth of features in one app.** Image creation and editing, voice conversations, deep research, custom GPTs and a large ecosystem.
- **Familiarity.** Most staff have already used ChatGPT, which lowers the training effort.
- **Custom GPTs.** Easy to build a focused assistant (for example a "quote checker") and share it with the team.
- **Consumer-friendly apps** across desktop and mobile.

## What matters more than the model

### 1. Data controls

The biggest real-world risk is not which model you pick. It is staff pasting customer data into personal accounts. Both providers offer business plans with admin controls where your content is not used for model training by default. Choose one, give everyone access, and write a short policy. Our guide on [AI data privacy for UK businesses](/resources/ai-data-privacy-uk-business) explains what to include.

### 2. The apps it needs to work with

Check which of your daily tools each assistant can connect to (for example Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack or your file storage) on the plan you are considering. For systems neither connects to directly, such as most job management software, the answer is an automation layer rather than a chat feature. See [how to connect two business apps](/resources/connect-business-apps).

### 3. How your team actually works

- A team that lives in Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace should test each assistant inside those tools.
- A sales team that wants one shared "proposal writer" may value custom GPTs or shared Projects.
- A development team will care most about the coding agent and editor integrations.

## A simple way to decide in one week

1. **List five real tasks** your team does every week, for example: reply to a complaint, summarise a supplier contract, draft a quote follow-up, analyse last month's jobs spreadsheet, write a job advert.
2. **Run each task in both tools** with the same prompt and material.
3. **Score each output** from 1 to 5 on accuracy, usefulness and how much editing it needed.
4. **Check the practicalities:** price for your headcount, admin controls, integrations you need.
5. **Pick one as the standard.** Allow the second for specific roles if there is a clear reason.

Use the same well-structured prompt in both. Our [prompt writing guide](/resources/prompt-engineering-guide) has a template.

## Our view

At Calon AI we use both Claude and ChatGPT models, alongside others, in the systems we build. We choose per task: the model that is most accurate and cost-effective for, say, classifying enquiries is not always the one we would use for drafting proposals. For a chat assistant your whole team uses, consistency and data controls matter more than small quality differences.

If you are moving beyond chat, for example to have AI qualify leads, answer enquiries or update your CRM automatically, the choice of model becomes a technical decision inside a workflow.
