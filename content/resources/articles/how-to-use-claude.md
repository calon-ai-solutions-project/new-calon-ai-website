> **Quick answer:** Sign up at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), type a request and attach any files it should work from. For ongoing work, create a **Project** with your reference files and instructions. When you want a document, dashboard or small tool you can keep, ask Claude to make it as an **Artifact**.

Claude is the AI assistant made by Anthropic. It is widely used for writing, analysis, research and coding, and is known for handling long documents well and following detailed instructions closely. This guide gets you productive with it quickly.

## Getting started

1. Go to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai), or download the Claude app for Windows, Mac, iPhone or Android.
2. Sign up with your email address or a Google account.
3. Type your request in the message box. Attach files with the **+** button.
4. Start a **new chat** for each unrelated task. Shorter, focused chats give better answers and use less of your usage allowance.

## Choosing a plan

The plans as listed on Anthropic's [pricing page](https://claude.com/pricing) in November 2026:

| Plan | Price (US list price) | Best for |
|---|---|---|

| Pro | $20/month, or $17/month billed annually | Regular individual use. More usage, Projects, Claude Code, more models. |
| Max | From $100/month | Heavy daily users. Five times Pro usage, priority access at busy times. |
| Team | $25 per standard seat per month, or $20 billed annually | Businesses of 2 to 150 people. Admin, SSO, shared projects, no training on your content by default. |
| Enterprise | Seat price plus usage | Larger organisations needing audit logs, SCIM, custom retention and compliance tooling. |

UK prices are shown in pounds at checkout and may include VAT. Plans change regularly, so always confirm on the official page.

Every plan has usage limits that reset every five hours, plus a weekly limit. Claude warns you when you are close. Long conversations, large files and the most capable models use your allowance faster.

## How to write a good request

Claude responds well to clear, structured instructions. Include:

- **Context:** who you are and what the output is for.
- **The task:** what to produce.
- **Material:** paste text or attach the file.
- **Rules:** tone, length, what to avoid.
- **Format:** bullets, a table, a one-page memo.

For longer requests, wrapping material in labels such as `<contract>...</contract>` helps Claude keep your instructions separate from the content. Our [prompt writing guide](/resources/prompt-engineering-guide) has templates you can copy.

## Projects: your workspace for ongoing work

A **Project** is a self-contained workspace with its own chats, knowledge and instructions. Use one for anything you come back to: a client, a bid, a product launch, your company policies.

### How to set up a Project

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar and create a new project.
2. Give it a clear name and a short description.
3. Add **project knowledge**: upload documents, paste text or add files Claude should use as background.
4. Add **project instructions**: how Claude should behave in this project, for example "You are helping write tender responses for a UK fire and security installer. Use UK English. Always reference our accreditations from the attached profile."
5. Start chats inside the project. Each one automatically uses the knowledge and instructions.

### Limits worth knowing

- Free accounts can create up to five projects.
- On paid plans, when project knowledge gets large Claude uses retrieval to search it, which lets a project hold far more material than fits in a single chat.
- Sharing projects with colleagues (view or edit) is available on Team and Enterprise plans.

## Artifacts: documents and tools you can keep

When Claude produces something substantial and standalone, such as a report, a slide deck, a dashboard, a diagram or a small interactive tool, it creates an **Artifact**. Artifacts open beside the conversation, where you can refine them by chatting, edit some types directly, export them or share them.

### How to turn Artifacts on

Artifacts depend on the file creation capability. Go to **Settings, then Capabilities**, and switch on the option for creating and editing files. On Team and Enterprise, an admin enables it under organisation settings.

### Good uses for Artifacts in a business

- A one-page proposal or policy you will edit and send.
- A simple calculator, such as a quote estimator for your sales team.
- A dashboard from an uploaded spreadsheet.
- A process diagram for onboarding new staff.

All your artifacts are saved in the **Artifacts** tab in the sidebar. They start private; you choose who can open them.

## Other features worth switching on

- **Web search:** lets Claude look up current information and cite sources. Check the citations for anything important.
- **Memory:** Claude can remember useful context across chats. Review it in settings, especially on shared devices.
- **Connectors:** on paid plans you can connect apps such as Google Drive or Microsoft 365, so Claude can work with your files directly. Only connect what your business has approved.

## Common problems and quick fixes

- **"Your message will exceed the length limit for this chat."** Attach fewer or smaller files, or start a new chat. Asking Claude to summarise the current chat and pasting the summary into a new one keeps the context.
- **"5-hour limit reached".** Wait until the reset time shown, or use usage credits if your plan has them.
- **Capacity messages.** These mean high demand, not an outage. Try again in a few minutes.
